# Primary Driver and Main Requirement

## Primary Driver

Dance event information is scattered across Facebook groups, WhatsApp chats, Instagram stories, and physical flyers. Dancers — especially newcomers — struggle to discover events and classes in their city, leading to lower attendance and a fragmented community. Organizers promote the same event across multiple channels individually, with limited visibility into reach or impact.

---

## Main Requirement

### Intended Outcomes

- Dancers discover events, classes, and community in their city from a single source
- Newcomers find their way into the dance scene without relying on insider knowledge
- Organizers reach their target audience effectively with visibility into impact
- Attendance grows as discovery friction decreases

### Enabling Conditions

- A platform where event information is aggregated and kept current per city
- The platform serves both dancers (discovery) and organizers (publishing + analytics)
- Event data is comprehensive enough to be the go-to source over scattered channels
