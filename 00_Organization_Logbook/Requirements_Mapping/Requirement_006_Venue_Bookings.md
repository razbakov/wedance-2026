# Requirement: Venue Connections

**Actor:** Venues
**Domain:** Marketplace

## Purpose

### Driver
Venues that host dance events rely on organizers finding them through personal connections or local reputation. There is no structured way for venues to signal availability, capacity, or suitability to the dance community — so good spaces go underused while organizers struggle to find affordable options.

### Requirement

**Venues** need to connect with organizers and dance communities so that they fill their spaces with dance events and reduce vacancy.

## Priority
Low — not in current validation scope

## Type
Operations

## Notes
Supply-side requirement. Venues benefit indirectly from increased event activity. Direct venue features are a later-stage concern.
