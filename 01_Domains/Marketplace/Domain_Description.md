# Marketplace — Domain Description (Delegation Canvas)

**Date/Version:** 2026-04-04
**Delegator:** Organization (see 03_Strategy.md)
**Review date:** 2026-07-04

## Purpose
**Primary Driver:** Artists (dance teachers, DJs) and venues have limited channels to connect with organizers and the broader dance community. Visibility depends on personal networks and word of mouth.
**Main Requirement:** A way for artists to get discovered and booked, and for venues to connect with organizers — strengthening the supply side of the dance ecosystem.

## Key Responsibilities
- Define artist profiles and visibility features (future)
- Define venue listing and connection features (future)
- Explore marketplace dynamics (supply/demand matching)

## Customers and Deliverables
| Customer | Deliverable |
|----------|-------------|
| Artists (teachers, DJs) | Visibility to dancers and organizers, booking opportunities |
| Venues | Connection to organizers and communities |
| Festival organizers | Access to artist talent pool |

## Key Challenges
- Classic chicken-and-egg marketplace problem
- Artists and venues have different needs than dancers — different product surfaces
- Monetization model unclear (commission? subscription? listing fees?)
