## Pipeline Stages

| Stage | Definition | Exit criteria |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **Researched** | Festival identified, organizer contact found | Research doc complete, fit score assigned |
| **Outreach drafted** | Personalized message prepared for Kirill | Draft reviewed by Kirill |
| **Contacted** | Kirill has sent the outreach message | Response received (positive or negative) |
| **Pitched** | Value proposition presented (call, meeting, or detailed message) | Organizer expresses interest or declines |
| **Piloting** | Agreement to run WeDance at their festival | Pilot complete, data collected |
| **Active** | Ongoing partnership post-pilot | Repeat partnership confirmed |
| **Declined** | Organizer declined or no response after follow-up | Documented reason, revisit date set |

## Blockers and Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Kirill's network check delayed | Cannot prioritize outreach | Escalate clearly; provide all options so Kirill can quick-scan |
| June festivals too tight for B2B | Miss early summer window | Run B2C experiment independently (per strategy) |
| Organizer sees WeDance as competition | Kills deal | Lead with "keep your ticketing, we just do schedule" messaging |
| Festival schedule not published yet | Cannot do B2C conversion | Monitor festival websites weekly for schedule publication |
| First pilot fails | Lose momentum | Select backup festival in same time window |
