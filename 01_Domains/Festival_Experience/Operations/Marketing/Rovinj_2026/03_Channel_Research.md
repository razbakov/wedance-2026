## Attendee Profile

Summer Bachata Festival in Rovinj, Croatia draws from:

1. **Croatia and the Balkans** -- local dancers, short travel distance
2. **Germany, Austria, Switzerland (DACH)** -- largest European bachata community, strong festival travel culture
3. **Italy** -- Rovinj is 2-3 hours from Venice/Trieste, northern Italian dancers attend Croatian coast festivals
4. **Slovenia** -- borders Croatia, active bachata scene in Ljubljana
5. **Hungary** -- Budapest has a growing bachata scene, 4-5 hour drive
6. **International festival circuit** -- dedicated bachata travelers from across Europe
