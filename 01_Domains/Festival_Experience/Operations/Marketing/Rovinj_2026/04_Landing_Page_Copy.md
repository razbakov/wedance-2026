## Value Propositions

### Value Prop 1: Filter by style

**Headline:** Find your workshops in seconds
**Body:** Dancing bachata sensual but want to catch a dominicana workshop too? Filter the full schedule by the styles you care about. No more scrolling through the entire lineup to find the workshops that match your level and taste.

---

### Value Prop 2: Works on your phone

**Headline:** Built for the festival hallway
**Body:** You are going to check this between workshops, standing in a Rovinj hotel lobby, probably holding a coffee. It loads fast, works on any phone, and you do not need to download anything. Just open and browse.

**Icon concept:** Phone screen showing the schedule, held at a casual angle. Maybe with a hint of Adriatic blue in the background.

### Value Prop 3: Always up to date

**Headline:** No more "wait, did they change Room 3?"
**Body:** Room swaps, time changes, last-minute additions -- the interactive schedule updates so you always have the latest version. No more comparing three different screenshots in the WhatsApp group.

**Icon concept:** Refresh arrow or clock with a checkmark.

## Tone Notes

- Dancers clicking through from a friend's WhatsApp message are already interested. Do not oversell.
- Keep it short. They want to see the schedule, not read copy. One scroll or one tap to the schedule.
- No corporate language. No "innovative platform" or "next-generation technology." It is a schedule that works better than a screenshot. That is the pitch.
- Rovinj is a vacation destination. The tone can be slightly more relaxed and warm than a generic festival page. Think "plan your beach-and-bachata weekend" energy.
- The visual design should feel like a tool, not a landing page. Minimal, mobile-first, fast.
- Language: English only. The festival is international.
