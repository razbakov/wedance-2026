## Value Propositions

### Value Prop 1: Filter by style

**Headline:** Find your workshops in seconds
**Body:** Dancing bachata and kizomba but skipping salsa? Filter the full schedule by the styles you care about. No more scanning through 40+ workshops to find the 12 that matter to you.

**Icon concept:** A filter/funnel icon with dance style tags flowing through it.

---

### Value Prop 2: Works on your phone

**Headline:** Built for the festival hallway
**Body:** You are going to check this between workshops, standing in a hallway, holding a coffee. It loads fast, works on any phone, and you do not need to download anything. Just open and browse.

**Icon concept:** A phone screen showing the schedule grid, held at a casual angle.

---

### Value Prop 3: Always up to date

**Headline:** No more "wait, did they change Room 3?"
**Body:** Room swaps, time changes, last-minute additions -- the interactive schedule updates so you always have the latest version. No more comparing three different screenshots in the group chat.

**Icon concept:** A refresh arrow or a clock with a checkmark.

## Tone Notes

- This page is for dancers who clicked a link from a friend's WhatsApp message or an SDTV Instagram story. They are already interested -- do not oversell.
- Keep it short. They want to see the schedule, not read marketing copy. The page should get them to the schedule in one scroll or one tap.
- No corporate language. No "revolutionary platform" or "cutting-edge technology." It is a schedule that works better than a PDF. That is the entire pitch.
- The visual design should feel like a tool, not a landing page. Minimal, mobile-first, fast.
