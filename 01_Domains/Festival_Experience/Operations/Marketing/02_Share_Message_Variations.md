**Date:** 2026-04-04
**Owner:** Marketing Lead (drafts) / Kirill (posts)
**Purpose:** Ready-to-use message templates for WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram. Swap in festival-specific details and the UTM-tagged link.

## UTM Parameter Guide

Use these UTM tags so every click is trackable:

| Channel | utm_source | utm_medium | utm_campaign |
|---------|-----------|------------|--------------|
| WhatsApp festival group | `whatsapp` | `message` | `{festival-slug}-launch` |
| WhatsApp personal DM | `whatsapp` | `dm` | `{festival-slug}-seed` |
| Facebook event page | `facebook` | `event` | `{festival-slug}-launch` |
| Facebook dance group | `facebook` | `group` | `{festival-slug}-{group-name}` |
| Instagram story (SDTV) | `instagram` | `story` | `{festival-slug}-launch` |
| Instagram reel (SDTV) | `instagram` | `reel` | `{festival-slug}-launch` |
| Instagram bio link | `instagram` | `bio` | `{festival-slug}` |
| YouTube community | `youtube` | `community` | `{festival-slug}-launch` |
| Direct message (personal) | `direct` | `dm` | `{festival-slug}-seed` |
