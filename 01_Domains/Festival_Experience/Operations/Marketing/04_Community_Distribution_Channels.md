# Community Distribution Channel Map

**Date:** 2026-04-04
**Owner:** Marketing Lead
**Purpose:** Identify, categorize, and locate the dance community channels where festival-goers gather -- so Kirill knows exactly where to share for any pilot festival.

---

## Channel Taxonomy

### Tier 1: Festival-Specific Channels (Highest Value)

These are groups created for a specific festival. Everyone in them is a confirmed or likely attendee. Conversion is highest here because the schedule is directly relevant.

| Channel Type | Platform | How to Find |
|--------------|----------|-------------|
| Official festival WhatsApp group | WhatsApp | Check the festival's Instagram bio, website, or email confirmation for a group link. Ask Kirill if he has been added to the group from a prior year. |
| Official festival Telegram group | Telegram | Same as above. More common for Eastern European and Russian-speaking festivals. |
| Festival Facebook event page | Facebook | Search Facebook Events for the festival name. The organizer usually creates this. |
| Unofficial attendee group | WhatsApp / Telegram | Created by dancers to coordinate travel/rooms. Ask known attendees or check festival comment sections. |
| Festival hashtag | Instagram | Search `#{FestivalName}`, `#{FestivalName}{Year}`. Follow and engage in the days before the event. |

**Action for each festival:** Before T-14, Kirill should confirm he has access to or can post in at least the WhatsApp group and Facebook event page. If not, identify a trusted community member who can share on his behalf.

---

### Tier 2: City Dance Community Channels

These are ongoing groups for dancers in the festival's host city. Members are not all attending the festival, but many will be -- and they influence others.

| Channel Type | Platform | How to Find |
|--------------|----------|-------------|
| City + style Facebook group | Facebook | Search: "{City} salsa", "{City} bachata", "{City} kizomba", "{City} social dance". Look for groups with 500+ members and recent activity. |
| City dance community WhatsApp | WhatsApp | Ask local dance school owners, instructors, or dancers Kirill already knows in that city. These are rarely discoverable via search -- they spread by word of mouth. |
| City dance Instagram accounts | Instagram | Search: `@salsa{city}`, `@{city}dance`, `@bachata{city}`. Look for accounts that post local event listings, not just personal dance videos. |
| Dance school accounts | Instagram / Facebook | The schools in the festival's city often post about the festival. Tag them, or ask them to re-share. |
| City event listing pages | Facebook / Instagram | Some cities have dedicated "events this weekend" accounts. In Germany: look for "Party {City}" or "{City} Events" pages. |

**How to prioritize:** Focus on groups where the festival's dance styles are the primary topic. A salsa festival should be shared in salsa groups, not general dance groups -- specificity drives relevance and click-through.

---

### Tier 3: Style-Specific National/International Groups

Broader communities organized by dance style rather than city. Lower conversion per post, but high reach and useful for festivals that attract international travelers.

| Channel Type | Platform | How to Find |
|--------------|----------|-------------|
| Style Facebook groups (national) | Facebook | Search: "Bachata {Country}", "Salsa lovers {Country}", "Kizomba Europe". Filter by member count and activity. |
| Style Facebook groups (international) | Facebook | Search: "Bachata festivals", "Salsa congress", "Kizomba addicts". These groups have 10K-100K members and are full of festival-goers. |
| Dance travel groups | Facebook | Search: "Dance travel", "Festival hopping", "Dance vacations". Members are exactly the type who attend festivals. |
| Style-specific influencer accounts | Instagram | Artists, instructors, and dance content creators who specialize in the festival's styles. They may re-share if tagged or if the tool is genuinely useful to their followers. |
| Dance YouTube channels | YouTube | Channels that cover festival recaps, workshop reviews, or "what to expect at" videos. SDTV is already the biggest one -- leverage that. |

---

### Tier 4: Kirill's Own Channels (SDTV)

The owned audience. Always part of the distribution plan, but used strategically to avoid audience fatigue.

| Channel | Audience Size | Best Format | Frequency |
|---------|--------------|-------------|-----------|
| SDTV Instagram | 510K followers | Stories (link sticker), Reels (screen recording) | 2-3 stories per festival, 1 reel max |
| SDTV YouTube | 133K subscribers | Community tab post with poll | 1 post per festival |
| SDTV Facebook | 548K followers | Page post with image | 1 post per festival |
| Kirill's personal accounts | Varies | Casual share, DM seeding | As needed |

**Caution:** SDTV content should feel like Kirill sharing something useful, not like an ad for WeDance. The schedule is a tool he is recommending to his audience, not a product he is selling.

## How to Research Channels for a New Festival

Follow this checklist when preparing distribution for a new pilot:

### Step 1: Start with the festival itself

- [ ] Visit the festival's website, Instagram, and Facebook page
- [ ] Look for official WhatsApp/Telegram group links (bio, pinned posts, email footers)
- [ ] Check if the festival has a Facebook event page (search Facebook Events)
- [ ] Note the festival's hashtags and any branded hashtags
- [ ] Identify the dance styles featured (salsa, bachata, kizomba, zouk, etc.)

### Step 2: Map the host city

- [ ] Search Facebook for "{City} + {each dance style}" groups
- [ ] Search Instagram for `@{city}{style}` and `#{city}{style}` accounts
- [ ] Ask Kirill: "Do you know anyone in {City}'s dance scene?" -- one connection unlocks WhatsApp groups
- [ ] Identify the 2-3 biggest dance schools in {City} (they run the community)
- [ ] Check if there is a local dance events listing page/account

### Step 3: Map the style communities

- [ ] Search Facebook for "{Style} festivals" and "{Style} lovers" groups
- [ ] Identify international groups where festival-goers from multiple countries gather
- [ ] Note which artists/teachers at the festival have large followings -- tag them

### Step 4: Assess access

For each channel found, answer:

| Question | If Yes | If No |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Is Kirill already a member? | He can post directly | Needs to join or find an insider |
| Is the group moderated (no promo)? | Frame as utility, not promotion | Post freely |
| Does Kirill know someone active in the group? | Ask them to share | Post himself or skip |
| Is the channel active (posts in last 7 days)? | Worth targeting | Skip -- dead group |

## Language Considerations

| Festival Region | Primary Language | Notes |
|----------------|-----------------|-------|
| Germany | German + English | Most dance groups are bilingual. Post in English unless the group is clearly German-only. |
| Spain / Latin America | Spanish | Translate core messages. Kirill may need a native speaker to review. |
| France | French | French dance groups rarely use English. Translate or skip. |
| Poland / Eastern Europe | English or local | International festivals use English. Local groups use local language. |
| Global / Online | English | Default for international groups. |

---

## Key Insight

The highest-converting channel is not the one with the most members -- it is the one where the audience is already going to this specific festival. One message in a 200-person festival WhatsApp group will outperform a post in a 50,000-member Facebook group every time.

**Priority order for every festival:**
1. Festival-specific WhatsApp/Telegram group
2. Festival Facebook event page
3. City dance groups (matching styles)
4. SDTV channels
5. International style groups

Everything else is bonus reach.
