## Fit Assessment

### Ideal Festival Profile
- 100+ attendees (enough to seed 10 free + 40 paid)
- Multi-day (2-4 days) — more coordination value
- Social dance styles (salsa, bachata, kizomba, zouk, timba)
- European location (timezone alignment, Kirill's circuit)
- Organizer accessible (warm intro via Kirill or Amado preferred)
- Shares schedule via PDF/Instagram (WeDance replaces this)

3. **Salsa Open Berlin** — Already in WeDance app data, 500+ attendees, major German city
