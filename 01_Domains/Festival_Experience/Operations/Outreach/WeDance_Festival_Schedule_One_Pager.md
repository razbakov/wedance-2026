# WeDance Interactive Festival Schedule

**A free tool that turns your workshop schedule into a personalized experience for every dancer.**

---

## The problem every festival organizer knows

You spend weeks planning your workshop schedule. You publish it as a PDF, an Instagram story, or a printed poster. Then during the festival, your inbox fills up with:

- "Which room is the bachata sensual workshop in?"
- "Can you send the Saturday schedule again?"
- "My friend wants to go to the same workshops as me -- what time is..."

Dancers screenshot the schedule, lose it, and ask again. They miss workshops they wanted to attend. They can't coordinate with friends. And you have no idea which workshops are actually generating the most interest until people show up (or don't).

---

## What WeDance does

You give us your schedule data. We give your ticket holders a link.

**For your attendees:**

| Feature | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| Filter by style | Dancers see only the workshops relevant to them (e.g., only Sensual, only Traditional) |
| Filter by time & room | "Show me everything in Room A on Saturday afternoon" |
| Personal schedule | Each dancer builds their own plan -- "my workshops for the weekend" |
| Share with friends | Send a link to a friend showing your selected workshops for easy coordination |
| Always current | If you change a room or time, the schedule updates -- no need to re-publish an image |

**For you as the organizer:**

| Feature | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| Workshop demand data | See which workshops are getting the most interest, before the festival starts |
| Gender balance signals | Lead/follow ratio per workshop -- useful for room and level planning |
| Attendance patterns | Understand which time slots are popular and which are underutilized |
| Fewer support messages | Dancers find their own answers instead of DMing you |
| Professional attendee experience | A polished, branded schedule tool that reflects the quality of your event |

## What we need from you

This is lightweight -- we designed it to require minimal effort from the organizer.

1. **Schedule data** -- Your workshop list with: name, instructor(s), time, room, style/level. A spreadsheet, a Google Doc, even a clear image -- we'll convert it.
2. **A link shared with ticket holders** -- Include the WeDance schedule link in your confirmation email, WhatsApp group, or Instagram story alongside your regular communication.

That's it. You keep your ticketing system. You keep your website. WeDance handles the schedule experience.

## FAQ

**Does this replace my ticketing system?**
No. WeDance handles the schedule experience only. Your tickets, registration, and payment stay exactly where they are.

**Do my attendees need to download an app?**
No. It works in the browser on any phone. Dancers just tap a link.

**What if I change the schedule last minute?**
Updates are instant. No need to re-publish an image or PDF. Dancers who saved a workshop that moved get notified.

**How do dancers access it?**
You share a link (in your confirmation email, WhatsApp group, or Instagram). Dancers open it, browse the schedule, and save their workshops. No account required to browse; optional sign-up to save a personal plan.

**What data do you collect?**
Only what dancers voluntarily provide: which workshops they're interested in, their style preferences. We follow GDPR. We never sell data. The analytics dashboard shows aggregated patterns, not individual dancer data.

Yes -- reach out to Kirill and we'll walk you through it live. Since we're both in Munich, a quick coffee meeting works perfectly.
