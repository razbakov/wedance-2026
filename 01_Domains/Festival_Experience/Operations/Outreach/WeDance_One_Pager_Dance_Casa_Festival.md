# WeDance Interactive Festival Schedule
## Customized for Dance Casa Festival 2026

**A free tool that turns your workshop schedule into a personalized experience for every dancer.**

---

## The challenge Dance Casa solves better than anyone -- and the one it can't (yet)

Dance Casa 2026 brings together 4 styles (Bachata, Salsa, Kizomba, Zouk), 35+ hours of workshops, multiple rooms, and dancers from 46 countries. That is an incredible achievement.

But here is the one thing no organizer has cracked: once you publish your workshop schedule, it becomes a static image that dancers screenshot, lose, and ask about again. With 4 styles and 4 rooms running in parallel, the scheduling complexity at Dance Casa is higher than most festivals. Dancers don't just need a schedule -- they need a way to navigate it.

That is what WeDance does.

## What we need from you

This is lightweight -- we designed it to fit into your existing workflow.

1. **Schedule data** -- Your workshop list with: name, instructor(s), time, room, style. A spreadsheet, Google Doc, or even photos of your planning draft. We convert it.
2. **Share the link** -- Include the WeDance schedule link alongside your regular communication to ticket holders (confirmation email, Instagram story, WhatsApp group).

That is it. You keep your ticketing. You keep your website. WeDance handles the schedule experience.

## FAQ

**Does this replace my ticketing system?**
No. WeDance handles the schedule experience only. Your tickets, registration, and payment stay exactly where they are.

**Do dancers need to download an app?**
No. It works in the browser on any phone. Dancers tap a link and go.

**What if I change the schedule last minute?**
Updates are instant. No need to re-post an image. Dancers who saved a workshop that moved get notified.

**What data do you collect?**
Only what dancers voluntarily provide: which workshops they are interested in, their style preferences. We follow GDPR. We never sell data. The analytics dashboard shows aggregated patterns, not individual dancer profiles.

**Can I see a demo?**
Yes -- Kirill can walk you through it on a quick video call. 15-20 minutes, no commitment.
