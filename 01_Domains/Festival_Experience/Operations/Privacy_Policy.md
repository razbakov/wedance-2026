## 1. What This App Does

WeDance Festival Schedule is a free web application that displays interactive workshop schedules for dance festivals. You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use it.

## 2. What Data We Collect

### 2.1 Analytics Events (via PostHog)

We use PostHog (PostHog Inc., San Francisco, CA, USA) to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us understand whether the interactive schedule is useful to dancers and improve the product.

**Events we track:**
- Page views and schedule opens
- Filter usage (which dance styles and days are selected)
- Share button taps and link copies
- Scroll depth and page performance metrics
- Return visits during the festival

**What these events contain:**
- A random anonymous identifier generated by PostHog (not linked to your name, email, or any account)
- Festival name and date (to distinguish between festivals)
- Event-specific properties (e.g., "filter_type: style, filter_value: bachata")
- Device type, browser, screen size, and operating system (automatically collected by PostHog)
- Approximate geographic region derived from IP address (PostHog processes IP addresses but we do not store raw IPs in event data)

**What these events do NOT contain:**
- Your name, email address, or phone number
- Any login credentials or account information
- Your precise location or GPS coordinates
- Any information you type into the app (there are no input fields)

### 2.2 UTM Parameters

When you arrive at the schedule via a shared link, the URL may contain UTM parameters (e.g., `utm_source=whatsapp`, `utm_medium=share`). These tell us which channel brought you to the schedule. UTM values are captured as properties on the page view event. They do not identify you personally.

### 2.3 Cookies and Local Storage

| Cookie / Storage | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|-----------------|---------|----------|------|
| PostHog anonymous ID | Distinguish unique visitors and detect return visits | 1 year | First-party cookie |
| PostHog session ID | Group events within a single browsing session | 30 minutes of inactivity | First-party cookie |

We do not use any third-party advertising cookies. We do not use tracking pixels. We do not run retargeting campaigns.

## 3. Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6)

We rely on **legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for collecting anonymous analytics data. Our legitimate interest is understanding whether dancers find the interactive schedule useful -- this is the core hypothesis we are testing.

We have conducted a balancing test and concluded that:
- The data collected is anonymous (no names, emails, or accounts)
- The processing is minimal (event counts and usage patterns only)
- The impact on your privacy is negligible
- You can opt out at any time (see Section 6)

If you are in a jurisdiction that requires consent for analytics cookies regardless of anonymity, we will display a cookie consent banner before any PostHog tracking activates.

| Data type | Retention period | Reason |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|

After the retention period, data is automatically deleted by the respective service.

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Even though we do not collect personal data in the traditional sense, you have the following rights under GDPR:

- **Right to information** -- This policy fulfills that right.
- **Right to access** -- You can request what data (if any) is associated with your anonymous PostHog ID.
- **Right to deletion** -- You can request deletion of your anonymous analytics data.
- **Right to object** -- You can object to analytics processing at any time.
- **Right to lodge a complaint** -- You can file a complaint with the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA): https://www.lda.bayern.de

**How to opt out of analytics:**
- Use your browser's "Do Not Track" setting -- PostHog respects this signal.
- Use a browser extension that blocks PostHog (e.g., uBlock Origin).
- Clear your cookies to reset your anonymous ID.

**How to exercise your rights:**
Email privacy@wedance.vip with your request. Since we do not collect identifying information, we may ask you to provide your PostHog anonymous ID (found in your browser's cookies as `ph_*`) to locate your data. We will respond within 30 days.

## 7. Data Transfers Outside the EU

## 8. Children
