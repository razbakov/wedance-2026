## Selection Criteria

Per strategy and Requirement 002, we are looking for:

- Multi-day dance festivals (salsa, bachata, kizomba, zouk)
- 200-1000 attendees (mid-size: large enough to validate, small enough for personal relationships)
- Located in Europe, ideally near Munich or where founders have connections
- Currently distributing schedules as static images (PDF, Instagram, printed)
- Multiple parallel workshops (creates real scheduling pain for dancers)
- Organizer contactable through public channels or Kirill's network
- Happening June-October 2026 (gives us 2+ months lead time to prepare)
