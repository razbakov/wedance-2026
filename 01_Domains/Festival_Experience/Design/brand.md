# WeDance Brand

## Logo

See `logos/` for assets.

- **Light background**: "We" in red (`#F1023D`), "Dance" in black
- **Dark background**: "We" in red (`#F1023D`), "Dance" in white
- **Never**: logo on a red background

Appears at the bottom of all posters and print materials.

## QR Codes

- Dynamic QR (trackable, changeable destination)
- WeDance logo embedded in center
- Test readability at 1 meter distance
- Unique UTM per placement: `utm_content=bathroom`, `utm_content=entrance`
