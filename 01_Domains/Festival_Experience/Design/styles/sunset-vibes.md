# Sunset Vibes

Warm, illustrated style for posters and social ads. Tells an emotional story — the feeling of heading to a festival with your crew.

## Mood

Warm, joyful, adventurous, inclusive, golden-hour

## Color Palette

| Color             | Hex       | Usage                              |
| ----------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Deep Purple**   | `#1A0A2E` | Background base                    |
| **Violet**        | `#4A1942` | Background gradient mid, footer bar |
| **Warm Cream**    | `#F5D5A0` | All text                           |
| **Sunset Orange** | `#E8854A` | Illustration accents, warm glow    |
| **Sunset Pink**   | `#D4627A` | Sky tones, illustration highlights |
| **Coral**         | `#E07850` | Skin tones, illustration warmth    |

### Rules

- No WeDance Red — warmth replaces urgency
- No white or black text — warm cream only
- Background gradient: deep purple (top) → violet → sunset tones (behind illustration)

## Typography

| Use          | Font              | Weight  |
| ------------ | ----------------- | ------- |
| Headlines    | **Serif/Display** | Bold    |
| Subheadlines | **Sans-serif**    | Regular |
| CTA text     | **Sans-serif**    | Medium  |
| Footer       | **Sans-serif**    | Bold    |

- Headline: large, centered, warm editorial feel, 3-4 lines
- Subheadline: noticeably smaller and lighter
- Thin horizontal line separates subheadline from CTA
- All text in warm cream (`#F5D5A0`)

## Imagery

- **Custom digital illustrations** — cartoon/editorial style, not photography
- **Sunset tones** — oranges, pinks, purples, warm skin tones
- **Diverse characters** — different ethnicities, genders, body types
- **Social situations** — the scene matches the headline (ride = car, dinner = table)
- **Expressions** — laughing, relaxed, connected — not posed
- **Sunset sky** visible through or behind the scene
- Illustration fills top ~45% of poster, bleeds to edges

### Rules

- Style should feel hand-drawn/artful, not stock or 3D-rendered
- Warm sunset lighting unifies every illustration
- No product screenshots, no UI mockups, no photos

## Layout Principles

- Dark purple gradient background, full bleed
- Illustration at top, text below
- Headline large and centered, dominates the text area
- QR code: light/white on dark background
- Footer: cream text on slightly darker purple bar at bottom

## Do / Don't

### Do

- Dark purple gradient backgrounds
- Warm cream/gold text only
- Custom illustrations with sunset palette
- Diverse, joyful characters in social moments
- Thin line separator before CTA
- Let the illustration lead (logo optional)

### Don't

- White or light backgrounds (use Clean Studio)
- WeDance Red for accents (use Bold Red)
- Stock photography or product screenshots
- Cold or blue-toned color schemes
- Small or decorative headlines — go big
