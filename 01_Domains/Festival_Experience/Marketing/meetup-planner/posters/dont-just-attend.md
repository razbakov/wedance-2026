# Poster — "Don't Just Attend"

## Concept

Product-focused poster that shows what WeDance does rather than selling with a hook. Clean, white, modern — lets the app screenshots and feature icons speak for themselves. Designed for dancers who want to understand the product at a glance before scanning the QR code.

## Copy

**Badge (green pill):**

> Free for the first 10 dancers

**Headline (large, bold, black):**

> Do not just attend. Connect.

**Subheadline (gray, regular weight):**

> Meet dancers. Join group dinners. Find ride shares. All around your next festival.

**CTA button (dark navy pill, white text):**

> Join [Festival Name]

**URL:** wedance.vip

**Footer:**

> Rides  Rooms  Dinners  Partners  Adventures

**QR code** → landing page with UTM `?utm_source=poster&utm_campaign=[festival-slug]&utm_content=[location]`

## Design Specs

| Element     | Value                                     |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Size        | A3 (297×420mm) or A4 (210×297mm)          |
| Orientation | Portrait                                  |
| Background  | White                                     |
| Headline    | Poppins Bold, black, ~48pt (A3)           |
| Subheadline | Poppins Regular, gray (#6B7280), ~20pt    |
| Badge       | Green (#48BB78) pill, white Poppins Bold  |
| CTA button  | Dark navy (#1E293B) pill, white text      |
| QR code     | Black on white, WeDance logo in center    |
| Icons       | Black line-style, consistent stroke width |
| Placement   | Entrance, chill zone, hotel lobby         |
| Print qty   | 5-10 per festival                         |

## App Screenshots

Three phone mockups shown side by side with slight overlap and rotation:

| Position | Screen              | Content                                                    |
| -------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Left     | Dancer profile      | Photo, name "Isabella", dance style, profile/ride/partners tabs |
| Center   | Group dinner        | Food photo, "Friday Dinner", 4 of 6 spots filled, progress bar, avatars |
| Right    | Activity detail     | "Beach Social Dance", date, description, extra activities link |

- White phone frames with subtle drop shadows
- Left phone rotated ~5° counterclockwise
- Center phone straight, slightly elevated
- Right phone rotated ~5° clockwise
- Center phone overlaps the other two

## Icon Row

Six line-style icons with two-line labels below each:

| Icon              | Label            |
| ----------------- | ---------------- |
| Two people waving | Meet dancers     |
| Car               | Share a ride     |
| Group of people   | Group dinners    |
| Taxi front view   | Taxi dancers     |
| Camera on tripod  | Videographer     |
| Sparkle suitcase  | Extra activities |

- Arranged in a single horizontal row, evenly spaced
- Black icons on white, no fill
- Labels centered below each icon

## CTA Section

Three elements on one horizontal line:

1. **Button** (left) — dark navy (#1E293B) fully-rounded pill, white bold text: "Join [Festival Name]"
2. **QR code** (center) — black modules, WeDance logo embedded in center
3. **URL** (right) — "wedance.vip" in bold black
