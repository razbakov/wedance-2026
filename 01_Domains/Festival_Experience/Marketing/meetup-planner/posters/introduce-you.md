# Poster — "Introduce You"

## Concept

Dance is the connector — the shared reason everyone is here. WeDance just makes the introductions that the festival floor can't. Positions dance as the bond, not something to compete with.

## Copy

**Headline (large):**

> Dance brought you here. We'll introduce you to each other.

**Subheadline:**

> Carpools, roommates, group dinners, and adventures — all matched with fellow festival dancers.

**CTA:**

> Scan to meet your crew for [Festival Name]

**QR code** → landing page with UTM `?utm_source=poster&utm_campaign=[festival-slug]&utm_content=[location]`

**Footer:**

> Rides · Rooms · Dinners · Partners · Adventures

## Design Specs

| Element     | Value                                  |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Size        | A3 (297×420mm)                         |
| Orientation | Portrait                               |
| Background  | Dark (stands out on white venue walls) |
| Font        | Bold sans-serif, high contrast         |
| QR code     | Bottom-right, ~8cm                     |
| Colors      | Brand colors + white text              |
| Placement   | Entrance, registration desk, lobby     |
| Print qty   | 5-10 per festival                      |
