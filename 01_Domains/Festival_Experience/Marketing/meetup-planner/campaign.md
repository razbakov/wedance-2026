# Launch Campaign

### Campaign Name: **"More Than Just Dancing"**

**Core message**: The best festival memories happen _between_ the workshops. Don't leave them to chance.

---

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch Buzz (2 weeks before pilot festival)

**Goal**: Fill 10 free spots fast, create FOMO

### Content Pillars

| Pillar            | Format                  | Channel           | Frequency    |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------- | ------------ |
| Pain point        | Short-form video/reels  | Instagram, TikTok | 3x/week      |
| Social proof      | Stories, testimonials   | Instagram Stories | Daily        |
| Urgency/scarcity  | Countdown posts         | All channels      | Every 2 days |
| Behind the scenes | BTS of matching process | Stories           | 2x/week      |

### Pain Point Posts (hook → agitate → solve)

1. **"Going to [Festival] alone?"** — You booked tickets, maybe a hotel. But you're eating alone, commuting alone, and hoping you'll randomly meet cool people. WeDance matches you with other dancers for rides, rooms, dinners, and meetups. First 10 spots are free.
2. **"The festival ends at midnight. Then what?"** — After-parties, beach meetups, city tours — all organized by dancers, for dancers. Join the crew going to [Festival].
3. **"Still looking for a roommate for [Festival]?"** — Stop scrolling Facebook groups. WeDance matches you with compatible roommates in 24h. Free for the first 10.
4. **"You'll take 200 workshops. You'll remember 1 dinner."** — The people you meet > the moves you learn. WeDance organizes group dinners at festivals. 4-6 strangers, one restaurant, zero awkwardness.

### Urgency Mechanics

- **Countdown graphic**: "7 free spots left" → "3 free spots left" → "LAST FREE SPOT"
- Update daily in stories
- Pin the counter to the landing page
- Each new sign-up triggers a story: "Welcome [Name] from [City]! 6 spots left"

### Phase 2: Seeding & Activation (1 week before)

**Goal**: Organic spread through existing dance communities

### WhatsApp/Facebook Group Playbook

**Message template** (adapt per group):

> Hey everyone! Going to [Festival]? 🎶
>
> We're organizing group activities for dancers — rides, roommates, group dinners, city tours, and more.
>
> First 10 spots are FREE → [link]
>
> Already [X] dancers signed up from [City1], [City2], [City3]...
>
> Who's in? 💃🕺

**Rules**:

- Post once, don't spam
- Get a group admin or known member to post (not a stranger)
- Follow up with "Update: only X spots left" 3 days later
- Never hard-sell — be helpful, not promotional

### Ambassador Strategy

Recruit **3-5 ambassadors** per festival who:

- Are already going
- Have social media presence in the dance scene
- Get free access + "Ambassador" badge + branded t-shirt to wear at the festival
- Share their unique referral link
- Post 1 story per week minimum

**Ambassador incentive**: If 5+ people sign up through their link, they get a free group dinner experience at the next festival.

### Print Material Prep

- Print **A3 posters** (5-10 per festival) and **table tent cards** (10-15) — see `docs/posters/` for designs
- Set up **dynamic QR codes** (e.g., QR.io, Bitly) with per-location UTMs: `utm_content=bathroom`, `utm_content=entrance`, etc.
- Add your logo in the center of the QR — test at arm's length (~1 meter) before printing
- Contact festival organizer for poster permission (offer co-branding: "powered by [Festival]")

### Phase 3: Launch Week (festival week)

**Goal**: Convert free users to paid, generate content for next festival

### Real-Time Content Machine

| Moment              | Content                                 | Who posts             |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Ride share meetup   | Photo of carpool crew                   | Participant + WeDance |
| Group dinner reveal | "We just found out where we're eating!" | Participant story     |
| During dinner       | Candid group photo                      | WeDance story         |
| After dinner        | "Met these amazing people" quote        | Participant           |
| Morning after       | "Breakfast with my new festival crew"   | Organic               |

### Day-Of Messaging

- Morning: "Tonight's dinner group has been matched! Check your DMs 🍽️"
- 2h before: "Your restaurant will be revealed in 2 hours..."
- 30min before: Restaurant reveal + map link
- After: "How was it? Rate your experience" → collect NPS + testimonial

### On-Site Poster Placement

| Location                         | Why it works                                      |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Entrance / registration desk** | Everyone passes through, high dwell time in queue |
| **Bathrooms (mirrors/doors)**    | Captive audience, people check phones here        |
| **Water/chill-out area**         | Resting dancers scrolling phones = ready to scan  |
| **Near schedule board**          | Already planning their time — add your activities |
| **Hotel lobby / hostel common**  | Catches travelers looking for roommates & rides   |

**Logistics**:

- Put posters up **Friday afternoon** before the first evening social
- Bring tape/blu-tack + spare posters (they get taken down)
- Check Saturday morning — replace any that fell/were removed
- Take photos of posters in-situ for social content

**Table tent cards** — if the festival has a lounge/bar with tables: front says "Scan for tonight's group dinner", back has QR + "WeDance — meet dancers beyond the floor". Cheap to print, high conversion.

### Paid Conversion Push

Once 10 free spots fill:

- "Free spots are gone! But for just €1 you're in → [link]"
- Show social proof: names, cities, activity count
- "12 dancers already in. Are you?"

### Phase 4: Post-Festival (1 week after)

**Goal**: Harvest content, build playbook for next festival

### Content Collection

- Send survey (Google Form): NPS, testimonial, photo permission
- Ask: "Would you use this at your next festival?"
- Request short video testimonials (15-30 sec)

### Retargeting Content

**Carousel post**: "What happened when 15 dancers did more than just dance at [Festival]"

- Slide 1: Hook — "They came for the workshops..."
- Slide 2: Stats — "3 carpools, 2 group dinners, 1 city tour"
- Slide 3: Quote — "[Testimonial]"
- Slide 4: Photo — group dinner candid
- Slide 5: CTA — "Next up: [Festival 2]. First 10 free → [link]"
