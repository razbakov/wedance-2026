## Release Slices

### MVP — Validate the hypothesis

One pilot festival. Manual matching. No payments. Goal: 10+ dancers sign up, 50%+ attend activities.

Stories: 1.1–1.4, 2.1–2.2, 3.1–3.3, 4.1–4.3, 5.1–5.4, 6.1–6.2, 8.1

### R1 — Scale to multiple festivals

Automated matching for rides and dinners. €1 paywall. Community-proposed activities. Retention loop.

Stories: 1.5–1.7, 2.3, 3.4, 5.5, 7.1–7.2, 8.2–8.3, 9.1–9.2

### R2 — Platform features

Taxi dancers, automated partner matching, organizer tools, persistent social groups.

Stories: 1.8–1.9, 2.4, 6.3–6.4, 8.4, 9.3–9.4
