# WeDance B2B Strategy — Festival Organizers

**Date**: 2026-03-19
**Status**: Hypothesis validation phase

## Vision

WeDance becomes the **solution partner for dance festival organizers** — working alongside their existing ticketing to provide dancer engagement, social coordination, and real-time analytics.

## Hypothesis

> Festival organizers will partner with WeDance if it gives them data they can't get elsewhere (workshop demand, gender balance, attendance) and reduces their support load — at no risk to their existing ticketing setup.

## Target Customer

**Dance festival organizers** (salsa, bachata, kizomba, etc.) who:

- Run multi-day festivals with 100+ attendees
- Share schedules via PDF and Instagram
- Coordinate with dancers through WhatsApp chaos
- Have no visibility into workshop demand or gender balance
- Want to promote their festival to dancers from similar events

## How It Works

1. **Organizer partners with WeDance** — keeps their existing ticketing system
2. **Shares the WeDance link** with ticket holders: "Plan your festival here"
3. **Dancers sign up** — browse the interactive schedule, mark workshops, find partners
4. **At the festival** — check in, find friends, coordinate on the fly
5. **After the festival** — stay connected, discover next events

## What Organizers Get

| Feature | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Gender balance data | Lead/follow ratio per workshop, before it starts |
| Workshop demand signals | Adjust rooms and times based on real interest |
| Attendance analytics | Actual participation, not just ticket sales |
| Promotion package | WeDance promotes the festival to dancers from similar events |
| Less support load | No more "where's the schedule?" DMs |

## What Makes It Different

- Not a ticketing platform — works alongside existing ticket systems
- Organizers get data they can't get anywhere else
- Style-specific (salsa ≠ tango ≠ hip-hop)

## Go-to-Market

1. **Partner with one organizer** — ideally one where we have connections
2. **Offer first festival free** — get a case study with zero risk
3. **Target one dance style** until critical mass
4. **After each festival**, promote the next ones to attendees — retention loop
5. **Scale**: repeat with proven playbook and case study

## Risk & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| No organizer wants to try it | Offer first festival completely free, zero risk |
| Organizer shares link too late | Provide launch timeline template, push for 2+ weeks before |
| Low dancer adoption | Partner matching is the hook — lead with that, not schedule replacement |
| Revenue timing is slow (only around festivals) | Fine early on — focus on validation, not revenue |
| Organizer sees WeDance as competitor to their ticketing | Clear positioning: we complement, never replace their ticket system |
