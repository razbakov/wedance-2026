# User Journey: Dancer at a Festival

## Entry Point

A dancer has already bought a ticket to a dance festival through the organizer's usual ticketing system. The organizer is a WeDance solution partner and shares a link with all ticket holders:

> "Plan your festival on WeDance — browse workshops, find dance partners, and coordinate with friends."

The dancer clicks the link. No app download required initially — it works in the browser.

## Step 1: Sign Up and Claim Your Spot

- Dancer opens the festival page on WeDance
- Signs up with name, dance style(s), and experience level
- Confirms their ticket (connects to the festival as an attendee)
- Sees the festival overview: dates, venue, schedule

**Goal:** Get in with minimal friction. Under 60 seconds.

## Step 2: Browse the Workshop Schedule

- Interactive schedule replaces the old PDF or Instagram grid
- Filter by dance style, level, teacher
- Tap a workshop to see details, teacher bio, and who else is going
- Mark workshops as "interested" or "going"

**Goal:** Replace the "screenshot the PDF and zoom in" experience.

## Step 3: Plan with Friends

- Share your workshop plan with friends already on the platform
- See which workshops your friends are attending
- Compare schedules side-by-side
- Create a shared plan: "We're all doing the Musicality workshop at 2pm"

**Aha moment:** "I can see what my friends are doing and we can plan together — no more WhatsApp chaos."

## Step 4: Find Dance Partners

- Tinder-style matching: swipe through other attendees
- Filter by dance style, role (lead/follow), and level
- Match before the festival — break the ice early
- Schedule a dance: "Let's dance at the social Saturday night"

**Goal:** Solve the "I don't know anyone" problem, especially for solo travelers.

## Step 5: At the Festival

- **Live radar** (opt-in): see where friends and matches are at the venue
- Check-in to workshops — organizer gets real attendance data
- Update your plan on the fly as things change
- Rate workshops and teachers

**Goal:** Make the festival feel organized and social, not overwhelming.

## Step 6: After the Festival

- See your festival stats: workshops attended, people met, dances shared
- Stay connected with new dance partners
- Get notified about upcoming festivals in your style
- Loyalty points accumulate toward free passes at future festivals

**Goal:** Turn a one-time festival visit into an ongoing relationship with the dance community.

## What the Organizer Gets

By adopting WeDance as a solution partner, the organizer receives:

- **Gender balance data** — know lead/follow ratio per workshop before it starts. When dancers match as partners and plan workshops together, organizers see exact pair counts, not just individual sign-ups
- **Dancer profiles** — see attendee levels, styles, and preferences. Spot that beginners are 60% of sign-ups and add a second beginners' session, or know that the advanced musicality workshop only has 8 people
- **Workshop demand signals** — see which sessions are overbooked vs empty, adjust rooms/times
- **Attendance analytics** — real participation data, not just ticket sales
- **Promotion package** — WeDance promotes the festival to dancers who attended similar events
- **Reduced support load** — no more "where's the schedule?" DMs

## Key Assumptions to Validate

1. Organizers will share the WeDance link with ticket holders (distribution depends on them)
2. Dancers will sign up before the festival, not just at the door
3. The partner-matching feature drives sign-ups (it's the hook beyond schedule browsing)
4. Post-festival retention is possible — dancers come back for the next event
5. Loyalty program is compelling enough to drive repeat usage
