As a Dancer, I want each ticket sold to appear as a real person on the event page, so that the attendee list I see is genuine and not padded with fake accounts.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The proof subhead explains that each ticket sold shows up as a face on the event page.
- The copy promises there are no fake friends or invented attendees.
- The attendee faces on an event page correspond to real people going.
- A dancer understands the count of attendees reflects actual ticket buyers.
