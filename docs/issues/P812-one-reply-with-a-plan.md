As a client, I want to describe my event in one message and get a plan back, so that I can forward a ready proposal to my partner or CFO without filling out long forms.

## Acceptance Criteria
- I can describe my event in a single message rather than a long form.
- The team replies with a plan for the event.
- The plan is clear enough to forward to a partner or a finance decision-maker.
