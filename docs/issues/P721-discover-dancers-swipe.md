As an organizer, I want my attendees to discover partners by swiping, so that finding people to dance with feels easy and playful and draws more dancers to the event.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Dancers can swipe through other dancers attending my festival to find partners.
- When two dancers both express interest, they become a mutual match.
- Mutual matches are collected into a dance list the dancer can revisit.
- The organizers toolkit presents discover-by-swipe as an included feature.
