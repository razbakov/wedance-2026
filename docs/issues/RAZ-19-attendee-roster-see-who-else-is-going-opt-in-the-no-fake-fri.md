# RAZ-19 — Attendee roster: See who else is going (opt-in) — the "no fake friends" promise

The flagship social-proof promise — currently **not built**. Underpins six landing-page claims across the home hero, proof section, and festivals page. Needs an opt-in attendee model tied to real accounts/tickets, a per-event roster UI, and privacy controls.
**Covers 6 landing promises — full stories below.**

---

### P205 — Home Proof: See who else is going

As a Dancer, I want the proof section to promise I can see who else is attending an event, so that I feel reassured I won't be walking in alone.

## Acceptance Criteria

* The proof section opens with a heading about seeing who else is going.
* The heading connects directly to the attendee lists shown on event pages.
* A hesitant dancer reads it as a reason to feel safe attending.
* The heading is visible as the lead of the proof section.

### P206 — Home Proof: Every ticket becomes a real face, no fake friends

As a Dancer, I want each ticket sold to appear as a real person on the event page, so that the attendee list I see is genuine and not padded with fake accounts.

## Acceptance Criteria

* The proof subhead explains that each ticket sold shows up as a face on the event page.
* The copy promises there are no fake friends or invented attendees.
* The attendee faces on an event page correspond to real people going.
* A dancer understands the count of attendees reflects actual ticket buyers.

### P207 — Home Proof: Who's coming shown per event

As a Dancer, I want each event to show a "Who's coming" list of real attendees, so that I can check the crowd before I decide to go.

## Acceptance Criteria

* The proof section shows an event card with a "Who's coming" area.
* The card displays real attendees for that specific event.
* Opening the event page shows the full attendee list.
* The attendees shown are people who added themselves as going or bought a ticket.
* Each event has its own separate attendee list.

### P211 — Attendee list: See who else is going, opt-in only

As a dancer, I want to see who else is going to a festival when they choose to share, so that I know familiar faces will be there before I commit.

## Acceptance Criteria

* A festival page shows people who have marked themselves as going.
* I only appear on the going list if I choose to share that I am going.
* I can mark myself as going or change my mind at any time.
* People who keep their attendance private are not shown to others.

### P302 — Home Hero: See who's going before you book

As a Traveler, I want to know I can see who is attending a festival before I buy, so that I feel confident I won't show up somewhere alone.

## Acceptance Criteria

* A caption under the hero button promises I can see who's going before booking.
* Following the festival link leads me to festival pages that show attendees.
* The caption clearly ties "who's going" to the decision to book.
* The caption is readable on phone and desktop.

### P306 — Festivals: See who is going before you book

As a traveling dancer, I want to see who is going to a festival before I book, so that I can decide based on who else will be there.

## Acceptance Criteria

* The Festivals page presents festivals mapped across locations.
* Each festival shows who has marked themselves as going.
* I can view a festival's attendees before deciding to book.
* I can mark myself as going so others can see me too.
