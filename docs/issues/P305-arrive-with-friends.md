As a traveler, I want to already know a few people before I arrive at a festival, so that I can walk straight onto the floor instead of standing alone against the wall.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Before the festival I can see and connect with other attendees who opted in.
- I can plan to meet up with those people at the event.
- When I arrive I have at least one familiar contact to dance with.
