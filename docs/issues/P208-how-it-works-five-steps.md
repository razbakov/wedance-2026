As a Dancer, I want the home page to explain how it works in five clear steps, so that I know what to expect before I sign up.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A "How it works" section is headed with a five-step, no-surprises promise.
- The section lays out the steps in order.
- Each step is short and easy to understand for a newcomer.
- After reading, a visitor knows what happens from arriving to getting value.
