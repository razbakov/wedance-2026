# RAZ-21 — Partner & connection matching — find a partner/friends before a festival

The whole partner/connection-matching layer — **not built**. Covers both the dancer side (find a partner / connect before a festival) and the organizer side (offer matching to lift ticket sales). Related to the attendee-roster issue ([WED-19](https://linear.app/wedance/issue/WED-19/attendee-roster-see-who-else-is-going-opt-in-the-no-fake-friends)).
**Covers 5 landing promises — full stories below.**

### P229 — Partner: Find a dance partner for a festival

As a dancer, I want to find a dance partner for a festival, so that I don't have to go alone and have someone to dance with.

## Acceptance Criteria

* Each festival on my plan shows a "Find a partner" action.
* I see other dancers going to the same festival who are looking for a partner.
* Each match shows the dancer's name, styles, and level.
* I can indicate that I'm looking for a partner at the festival.
* I can reach out to a potential partner.

### P236 — Shared Year: Offer to be their partner

As a dancer viewing a friend's year, I want to offer to be their partner at a festival, so that we can attend together.

## Acceptance Criteria

* When I view a friend's year, festivals show a "Be their partner" action.
* Choosing it lets my friend know I want to be their partner for that festival.
* The action is available for festivals they're planning to attend.
* My friend can see and respond to my offer.

*WSJF 1.2 · CUJ C4 · JTBD J4 · page* `/my-year` *· source: "Be their partner"*

### P304 — Trip planning: Organize dinners, rides and partners together

As a traveler, I want to coordinate the trip with other attendees, so that I can arrange dinners, share rides, and line up a first-night partner before I arrive.

## Acceptance Criteria

* From a festival page I can connect with others who are going.
* I can arrange to share a ride to the festival with another attendee.
* I can plan a shared dinner or meetup around the festival.
* I can find a partner for the first night before I travel.
* I only share my plans with people who have also opted in.

### P712 — Toolkit: Partner matching

As an organizer, I want my festival to offer partner matching, so that dancers can find someone to attend and dance with, which makes solo dancers more likely to buy a ticket.

## Acceptance Criteria

* My festival page presents partner matching as an available feature.
* Dancers going to my festival can indicate whether they lead or follow.
* Dancers can find potential partners among others attending my festival.
* The organizers page lists partner matching among the included tools.

### P720 — Toolkit: Leads and follows match early

As an organizer, I want leads and follows to find each other before my festival, so that solo dancers arrive with a connection and are more likely to attend.

## Acceptance Criteria

* Dancers attending my festival can mark whether they lead or follow.
* Leads can find follows and follows can find leads among attendees.
* Matching is available before the festival starts, not only on-site.
* The organizers toolkit lists partner matching as an included feature.
