As an organizer, I want my festival page to show the real dancers who are going, so that potential attendees see a genuine community and feel confident buying a ticket.

## Acceptance Criteria
- My festival page shows profile photos of dancers who marked themselves as going.
- The faces shown are real WeDance profiles, not stock images.
- A dancer who adds the festival to their plan appears among the attendee faces.
- Visitors can tap a face to view that dancer's profile.
