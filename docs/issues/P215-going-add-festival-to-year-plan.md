As a dancer, I want to mark a festival as Going, so that it is added to my year plan.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Each festival card shows a "Going?" prompt.
- When I tap it, it changes to "Going!" to confirm my choice.
- The festival is added to my year plan when I mark it as going.
- I can undo the choice to remove the festival from my year plan.
