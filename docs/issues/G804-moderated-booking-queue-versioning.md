As the elected moderator, I want to approve or decline free-booking requests against the active guidelines, so that confirmed slots follow the community's rules; and I want each term's guidelines versioned, so that history is preserved across elections.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The moderator sees incoming free-booking requests for the space.
- The moderator can approve or decline each request against the active guidelines; approved slots appear in "Scheduled".
- Guidelines are versioned per term, and past terms' rules remain viewable.
