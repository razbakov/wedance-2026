As a dancer, I want to see who else is going to a festival when they choose to share, so that I know familiar faces will be there before I commit.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A festival page shows people who have marked themselves as going.
- I only appear on the going list if I choose to share that I am going.
- I can mark myself as going or change my mind at any time.
- People who keep their attendance private are not shown to others.
