## Acceptance Criteria
- Each festival on my plan shows a "Share a room" action.
- I first see other dancers looking for a roommate at the same festival.
- Each roommate listing shows the person, dates, and what they're looking for.
- When no roommates are available, I see hotel options as a fallback.
- I can post that I'm looking for a roommate at the festival.
