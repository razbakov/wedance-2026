As a Dancer, I want the home page to promise that the dances, teachers, cities and people shown are real, so that I trust what I find here more than fake or padded listings elsewhere.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The hero subhead promises real dances, teachers, cities and people, not invented ones.
- The word "real" is emphasised so the anti-fake promise stands out.
- A skeptical visitor understands the site avoids fake profiles and padded numbers.
- The subhead sits directly under the main headline and reads as one clear sentence.
