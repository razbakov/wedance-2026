As a dancer, I want to filter the artists directory by my city, so that I can find teachers and performers who are actually near me.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The Artists page shows a city filter control.
- Selecting a city narrows the list to artists based in or active in that city.
- The chosen city stays selected while I browse the results.
- Clearing the filter returns me to the full artists list.
- When no artists match the selected city, I see a clear "no artists here yet" message.
