As a moderator candidate, I want to propose the guidelines the space would run by, so that the community elects me on the basis of the rules I stand for.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A candidate can attach a written set of guidelines to their candidacy.
- The proposed guidelines are visible to the community before voting opens.
- Each proposal is tied to its candidate and the election term.
