As a traveling dancer, I want to discover new people and places, so that my year can grow beyond what I already know and pull me somewhere new.

## Acceptance Criteria
- My plan has a "Widen your year · discover" section.
- It suggests people and places I don't already follow or plan around.
- Each suggestion explains why it might interest me.
- I can add a suggested festival or place to my plan.
- I can dismiss a suggestion I'm not interested in.
