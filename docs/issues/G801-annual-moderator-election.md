As a community using a free/OpenAir commons (e.g. the Pinakothek Open Air space), we want to elect a moderator each year, so that the community — not a platform admin — chooses who stewards the space.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A free/OpenAir space can open an annual moderator election with a clear start and end.
- Any eligible dancer with a real profile can stand as a candidate.
- The election runs for a defined one-year term.
- The result is recorded against the space, and the winning moderator is linked to their real profile.
