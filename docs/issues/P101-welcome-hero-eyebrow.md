As a visitor, I want the home page to greet me and instantly signal this is a place for dancers, so that I feel I've arrived somewhere made for me.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The home page opens with a welcoming eyebrow line above the main headline.
- The greeting makes it clear the site is about dance, not a generic events site.
- The welcome line is visible without scrolling on both phone and desktop.
- The tone feels inviting to a newcomer, not only to experienced dancers.
