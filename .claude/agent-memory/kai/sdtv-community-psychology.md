# SDTV — Community Psychology & Business Model Reality

last_updated: 2026-04-07
source: founder insight

## Social Dance Community Psychology

### How Festivals Think About Videographers
- Festivals **rotate videographers by design** — they want different camera styles each year
- Videographer = replaceable commodity in their eyes
- Even perfect delivery doesn't guarantee renewal
- Decision is made based on their plan, not your performance
- Real case: not yet delivered content, but next year already given to another videographer

### What Festivals Actually Need (But Don't Always Articulate)
- Visibility, ticket sales, community engagement, brand authority
- They NEED promo — but historically got it bundled cheap or free with production
- They're used to paying for cameras, not for reach

### Community Dynamics
- Offline events = heartbeat of the social dance world
- Community is tight-knit, reputation-driven
- Dancers follow content creators (SDTV channel) not just events
- SDTV channel IS a community touchpoint — not just a service provider's portfolio

## Current Business Model (Transitional State)

### How It Works TODAY
```
Production (core offer) → Promo as secondary addon
```
- Existing clients hired SDTV as videographers first
- Promo/distribution came bundled, often underpriced or free
- Clients perceive value as "filming" not "reach"
- This is the OLD model — still active with current client base

### Where It's Going
```
Promo (core offer) → Production as part of promo package
```
- Channel and audience = the real moat
- Production feeds content engine, not the other way around
- Not yet implemented with existing clients

### Critical Context for All Agents
**Current clients are still on old rules.** Do NOT pitch or communicate the new model to existing clients without founder approval. The transition needs to be gradual and strategic.

Key tension:
- New clients → can be onboarded with promo-first model
- Existing clients → expect production-first, promo-as-bonus
- Upselling promo to existing clients = requires reframing, proof, timing

## What This Means for Agent Behavior

### Kai (Community/Leads)
- New leads: qualify by promo value potential, not just production fit
- Existing contacts: don't reframe the relationship without founder guidance
- Track which clients value promo vs just want cameras

### Marco (Strategy/Deals)
- Price promo as the premium tier, not the addon
- For renewals: build case for promo value with data (views, reach, engagement)
- New deals: lead with channel reach, not filming quality

### Luna (Content)
- Every piece of content is a promo asset with business value
- Content strategy = growth strategy = revenue strategy
- Channel metrics directly impact deal pricing power

### Maya (Ops)
- When routing tasks: understand that "filming gig" ≠ full business value
- Promo delivery is as operationally important as production delivery
