# SDTV — Value Proposition Flip (Strategic Framework)

last_updated: 2026-04-07
source: founder reflection + strategic analysis

## Mantra

**Production is not the product. Production is the fuel. Promo is the product.**

## Core Problem

Festivals rotate videographers by design. They want different camera styles each year.
Even perfect delivery doesn't guarantee renewal.
**Real case:** content not yet delivered, but next year already given to another videographer.

If the model allows the client to perceive you as a replaceable production vendor — you will be replaced.

## The Moat

Camera → replaceable.
Editor → replaceable.
Photographer → replaceable.
**Channel with accumulated community trust, attention distribution, scene context, and existing dance audience → NOT replaceable.**

The main strategic mistake of the past: devaluing the scarcest asset (channel access to community attention).

## Old Model (Current Reality with Existing Clients)

```
PRODUCTION (core) → promo as addon/discount → sell packages
```

- Clients hired SDTV as videographers first
- Promo came bundled, often underpriced or free
- Clients perceive value as "filming" not "reach"
- Easy to replace, no switching cost, no moat
- Revenue depends on physical presence at events
- **Still active with current client base** — transition ahead

## New Model (Target)

```
PROMO & DISTRIBUTION (core) → production as supporting layer
```

Old positioning: "We film festivals and add promo."
New positioning: **"We help festivals stay visible, desirable, and talked about — before, during, and after the event. Production is one layer of that system."**

## Three Products

### 1. Promo / Distribution Product
Can be sold WITHOUT on-site presence.
- Collab campaign
- Countdown campaign
- Artist spotlight package
- Post-event visibility cycle
- Next edition hype package

### 2. Event Media Product
On-site physical delivery.
- Video coverage, photo, stories, recap
- On-site capture, live content

### 3. Visibility Partner Product
Annual or seasonal package (hardest to replace with "another camera"):
- Pre-event promotion
- Live event coverage
- Post-event distribution
- Next edition launch

## KPI Framework

### North Star
**Recurring Promo Revenue Share**
% of revenue from promo, distribution, collab-publications, seasonal/annual visibility deals (not one-off filming).
Goal: from 10-20% addon → core of business.

### Sustainability
**Revenue Independence Ratio**
`remote promo revenue / total revenue`
Growing = no longer hostage to travel calendar.

### Retention
**Client Renewal Rate** — % returning next cycle.
**Renewal Visibility Score** — does client have a clear report showing business impact BEFORE making next-year decision?
Without proof of value, renewal decision happens without your "proven worth."

### Monetization
**Promo-to-Production Attach Rate**
% clients who buy promo FIRST, then add production.
Flip the funnel: not "filmed + little promo" → "took visibility/promo + enhanced with production."

### Promo Quality (per client Impact Dashboard)
- Total reach
- Views
- Saves + shares
- Profile visits
- Follower growth during campaign window
- Outbound clicks / ticket clicks / code uses
- UGC repost volume
- Artist/festival tag network effect

### Commercial Leverage
**Average 12-Month Client Value**
Not cost of one trip, but value of one account per year:
pre-event promo + live coverage + post-event distribution + artist collabs + teaser next edition + ticket push + annual positioning package.

### Portfolio Health
**Share of clients on package/retainer deals**
% buying season/year package vs one-off service.

### Brand Asset
**Owned Audience Growth Quality**
Not just subscribers, but:
- Engagement rate
- Saves/share rate
- DMs from organizers
- Inbound partnership requests
- Festival invitations
- % leads that mention the channel itself
Question: is SDTV growing as a standalone media asset, or still selling hours of presence?

## Immediate Action Plan

1. **Rewrite offer** — not "video coverage" but "festival visibility system" / "media & promotion partner"
2. **Split pricing into 2 budgets** — Production budget + Promo/distribution budget
3. **Mandatory impact recap per client** — one-page report: coverage delivered, reach, engagement, profile effect, best-performing assets, recommendations for next edition
4. **Build renewal pipeline** — mid-cycle report → post-event review → proposal for next edition within fixed window. Don't wait for client to choose.
5. **3 business goals for 12 months:**
   - Raise promo revenue share
   - Raise repeat/renewal revenue
   - Reduce dependence on on-site days

## Transition Rules

- **Existing clients** → still on old rules (production-first, promo-as-bonus). Transition gradually, with founder approval only.
- **New clients** → can be onboarded with promo-first model from day one.
- **Upselling promo to existing** → requires reframing, proof (impact data), and right timing.

## Market Context

- Buyers push for measurable business outcomes
- Social and digital video among fastest-growing channels
- Community building = critical for social strategy
- Video spend moving toward scalable, measurable programs (not one-off "just film something")
- Measurement and proof of effect already part of the value proposition itself
