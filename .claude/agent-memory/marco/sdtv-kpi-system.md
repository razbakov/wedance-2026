# SDTV — KPI System 2026

last_updated: 2026-04-07
source: founder strategic framework

## Design Principles

- Core team = 2 people + designer (outsource) + editor (outsource)
- System must be LEAN: 1 North Star, 8 core KPI, 1 weekly, 1 monthly, minimal manual work
- NOT corporate — built for a 2-person team running like a larger company
- KPI answers: "Are we becoming less replaceable and more monetizable as a media partner?"

## North Star: Promo-Led Revenue

Revenue from visibility / promo / distribution products (not just filming).

**Includes:** promo packages, collab campaigns, artist/festival spotlight, pre-event hype, post-event visibility cycle, annual/seasonal visibility deals.
**Excludes:** pure filming, pure photo, travel reimbursements, technical one-offs without promo value.

**Target 2026:** +50% to +80% vs promo revenue 2025

## 8 Core KPIs

### 1. Promo Revenue Share
`Promo-Led Revenue / Total Revenue * 100`
- Target Q4 2026: 40-55%
- <30% = still production-heavy
- 30-40% = transition in progress
- 40%+ = model is changing
- Show in: Monday scorecard + monthly Notion card

### 2. Remote Revenue Ratio
`Remote/non-onsite revenue / Total Revenue * 100`
- Target 2026: 25-35%
- Remote = collab promo, campaign publishing, launch support, remote edit+distribution, repost/spotlight cycles, calendar promo packages
- Growing = less dependent on travel calendar

### 3. Client Renewal Rate
`Renewed clients / renewal-eligible clients * 100`
- Target 2026: 55-70%
- Only count those with real renewal window
- Don't count: one-off without next iteration, left market, paused for objective reasons
- Monday statuses: Eligible, Renewal Open, Renewed, Lost

### 4. Average 12-Month Client Value
`Total client revenue last 12mo / number of active clients`
- Target 2026: +25% to +40% vs 2025 base
- Stimulates: upsell promo, post-event cycles, next-edition teasers, artist tie-ins, seasonal packages
- Think "account value per year" not "earnings per trip"

### 5. Gross Margin %
`(Revenue - direct costs) / Revenue * 100`
- Target 2026: 45-60% overall, 60-75% for promo-only products
- Direct costs: editing, design, travel, accommodation, assistants, contractors, paid boosts, external production
- Critical for 2-person team: can grow revenue but drown on margin

### 6. Qualified Inbound Opportunities / Month
Count of inbound leads passing qualification (clear client, type fit, budget potential, real interest in visibility/promo/media partnership)
- Target 2026: 4-8 qualified inbound/month
- Track separately: channel-led inbound % (came because they saw SDTV as media channel)

### 7. Pipeline Coverage Ratio
`Weighted pipeline next 90 days / revenue target next 90 days`
- Target 2026: 2.5x-3.5x
- Weights: warm lead 20%, qualified discovery 40%, proposal sent 60%, verbal yes/negotiation 80%, booked 100%
- Don't wait last minute for festival bookings — see deal reserve ahead

### 8. On-Time Delivery Rate
`Deliverables on time / total deliverables * 100`
- Target 2026: 90-95%
- If delivery slips → renewal, trust, and upsell all suffer
- For small team this is commercial foundation, not just ops

## NOT Core (Diagnostic Only)

Keep as supporting metrics, not top-level KPI:
- Follower growth
- Reach by itself
- Views by itself
- Saves/shares without context
- Number of trips
- Hours filmed
- Reels published

## Red Flags 🚨

Act fast if you see:
- Promo Revenue Share < 30%
- Remote Revenue Ratio < 15%
- Renewal Rate < 45%
- Gross Margin < 40%
- Pipeline Coverage < 2.0x
- On-Time Delivery < 85%

= Business still too tied to one-off trips, not capitalizing the channel.

## Ownership Split

### Person 1 (Кирилл): Growth / Sales / Client Strategy
- Promo-Led Revenue
- Promo Revenue Share
- Renewal Rate
- Client Value
- Qualified Inbound
- Pipeline Coverage

### Person 2 (Wife): Delivery / Content Ops
- On-Time Delivery
- Gross Margin tracking
- Campaign results collection
- Impact reports
- Editor/designer coordination

### Outsource (editor/designer)
- Not KPI owners, but influence margin, delivery time, rework level
- Must be embedded in SLA

## Review Rhythm

### Weekly (20 min)
Look at 5 things only:
1. Promo Revenue Share
2. Pipeline Coverage
3. Qualified Inbound
4. On-Time Delivery
5. Urgent renewals

### Monthly (45-60 min)
All 8 KPIs + answer 4 questions:
1. Are we becoming more promo-led or not?
2. Are we less dependent on onsite or not?
3. Are clients returning more often or not?
4. Are margin and delivery holding or not?

### Quarterly (90 min)
Decisions:
- Which products to keep
- Which products to raise price on
- What to move to remote-first
- Which clients aren't worth the effort
