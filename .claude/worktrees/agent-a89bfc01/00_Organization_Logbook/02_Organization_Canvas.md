# WeDance — Organization Canvas

## Purpose

Strengthen the dance ecosystem by making events, classes, and community discoverable for dancers and reachable for organizers.

## Overall Strategy

See 03_Strategy.md (to be defined in Phase 2).

## Customers, Partners, and Stakeholders

**Customers:**
- **Dancers** — discover events, classes, and community in their city; coordinate social activities at festivals
- **Festival organizers** — reach dancers, publish schedules, get analytics (workshop demand, gender balance, attendance)

**Partners (to be validated):**
- Dance schools and studios
- Pilot festival organizer(s)

**Stakeholders:**
- Dance communities (local and international)
- Venue owners (indirectly affected by attendance)

## Business Model(s)

Not validated. Two hypotheses in testing:

1. **Festival Schedule** — Interactive digital schedule for dance festivals, replacing static images (printed/Instagram). Two paths to test: organizer-driven (B2B) vs dancer-driven (B2C). See [Requirements Mapping](Requirements_Mapping/).
2. **Meetup Planner** — Social coordination for dancers at festivals (rides, rooms, meals, partner matching). First 10 free → €1 per dancer. See [Requirements Mapping](Requirements_Mapping/).

Both target multi-day dance festivals (salsa, bachata, kizomba) as the entry point.

## Environmental Conditions

- **Legal/Economic:** GDPR compliance required (EU users). Bootstrapped — no external funding assumed.
- **Market trends:** Dance festival scene is growing post-COVID. Dancers already coordinate via WhatsApp/Facebook but tools are fragmented and informal.
- **Competition:**
  - Generic event platforms (Eventbrite, Meetup) — not dance-specific, no community features
  - Timeleft — social dining concept, not dance-specific
  - Festival-specific apps — schedule only, no social coordination or analytics
  - Facebook/WhatsApp groups — unstructured, no matching or analytics
