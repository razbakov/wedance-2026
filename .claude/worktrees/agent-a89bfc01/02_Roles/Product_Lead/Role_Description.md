## Purpose
**Primary Driver:** The partnership needs to translate product vision into concrete specs, user stories, and prioritized backlogs — faster than two busy founders can do alone.
**Main Requirement:** Turn strategic decisions into actionable product work, maintain the product backlog, and ensure experiments are well-defined before engineering starts.

## Key Responsibilities
- Translate strategy and requirements into user stories and specs
- Maintain and prioritize the product backlog
- Define acceptance criteria for each experiment
- Research competitors and market trends
- Draft product decisions for Alex's review
- Coordinate with Designer and Engineer on feature scope
