# Requirement: Artist Visibility and Bookings

**Actor:** Artists (dance teachers, DJs)
**Domain:** Marketplace

## Purpose

### Driver
Artists (dance teachers, DJs) depend on personal networks and word of mouth for visibility and bookings. Discovery happens by reputation within a niche, not by searchable presence — meaning talented artists outside established circles struggle to get booked, and organizers miss out on available talent.

### Requirement

**Artists** need visibility to dancers and organizers so that they get discovered and booked for events and festivals based on their skills and availability, not just personal connections.

## Priority
Low — not in current validation scope

## Type
Operations

## Notes
Supply-side requirement. Artists are a key part of the ecosystem but this is a marketplace problem — harder to solve and lower urgency than discovery. Revisit after Festival Experience validation.
