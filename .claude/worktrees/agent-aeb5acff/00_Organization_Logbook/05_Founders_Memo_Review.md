Hi Kirill, I reviewed the memo. Overall it's solid and I'm ready to sign — but I'd like us to address a few gaps before or shortly after signing. Here's what I found:

## Summary

The memo covers the basics well: decision-making, confidentiality, IP, expenses, departure. Nothing harmful or one-sided. But there are a few gaps worth fixing given that we've already been working together and have existing assets.

| # | Issue | Severity | Suggestion |
|---|-------|----------|------------|
| 1 | Pre-existing IP/assets not covered | High | Add Schedule A or one sentence covering existing assets |
| 2 | No governing law or dispute resolution | Medium | Add German law, Munich jurisdiction, mediation-first |
| 3 | "Reasonable notice" for departure undefined | Medium | Set minimum 60 days written notice |
| 4 | Non-compete has no time limit | Medium | Limit to 12 months after departure |

| 6 | No equity split mentioned | Low | Fine for now, but let's align on expectations |

---

## 1. Pre-existing IP (Section 4) — High

The memo only covers work "created after the date of this memo." But we already have: code repositories, domains (wedance.vip), brand assets, Figma designs, strategy docs, user data, cloud accounts, and AI agent configurations.

**Proposal**: Add to Section 4:

> "The founders acknowledge that project assets created before this memo — including but not limited to code repositories, domains, brand assets, design files, strategy documents, and cloud accounts — are jointly held project assets under the same terms as assets created after this date. A shared inventory of these assets is maintained separately."

Alternatively, we can attach a Schedule A listing everything.

---

## 2. Governing Law (Missing) — Medium

The memo has no governing law or dispute resolution clause. If we ever disagree, there's no agreed process.

**Proposal**: Add a new Section 10:

> "This memo is governed by the laws of Germany. Any disputes will first be addressed through good-faith discussion. If unresolved within 30 days, the founders agree to seek mediation before pursuing legal action. The courts of Munich, Germany shall have jurisdiction."

---

## 3. Departure Notice (Section 8) — Medium

"Reasonable written notice" is vague. Could mean 1 week or 6 months.

**Proposal**: Replace "reasonable written notice" with:

> "at least 60 days' written notice"

---

## 4. Non-Compete Time Limit (Section 5) — Medium

The restriction on not building a competing product has no expiry. As written, it could theoretically apply forever after someone leaves.

**Proposal**: Add to Section 5:

> "This non-use obligation applies during the collaboration and for 12 months after a founder's departure. A 'competing version' means a product substantially similar in function to the project's core offering at the time of departure."

---

## 5. Expense Threshold (Section 7) — Low

"Meaningful expense" is subjective. We're already running AI costs, cloud infra, domains, etc.

**Proposal**: Add:

> "Expenses above EUR 100 per month per item require advance discussion. The founders will maintain a shared expense log."

---

## 6. Equity Split — Low (for now)

The memo intentionally avoids stating a split. That's fine at this stage, but we should be aligned that our working assumption is 50/50, to be formalized at incorporation.

No change needed in the memo — just confirming we're on the same page.

---

## Suggested Approach

I'd like to propose:

1. **Before signing**: Add the pre-existing IP sentence (item 1) and governing law clause (item 2) — these are the most important.
2. **Sign the memo** with "WeDance" as project title.
3. **Within 30 days**: Amend to add items 3-5.
4. **Before incorporation or funding**: Engage a German startup lawyer for a proper GmbH setup.
