# Poster A — "Not a Lottery"

## Concept

Dancers spend serious money on festival travel hoping for magic — but it's a gamble. They want predictable connection, not random luck.

## Copy

**Headline:**

> Your festival trip shouldn't be a lottery.
> Go there already connected.

**Subheadline:**

> Match with dancers before you land. Rides sorted. Rooms shared. Dinners planned. People found.

**Body:**

> WeDance connects you with fellow festival-goers before you even pack your bag. No more solo taxis, random roommates, or eating alone. Your crew is waiting.

**CTA:**

> Scan to find your crew for [Festival Name]

**Footer:**

> Rides · Rooms · Dinners · Partners · Adventures
