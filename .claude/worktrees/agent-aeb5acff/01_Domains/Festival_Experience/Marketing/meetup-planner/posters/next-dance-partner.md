# Poster — "Next Dance Partner"

## Concept

Uses dance as the bridge to practical needs. Your next dance partner might also need a ride — the shared love of dance turns logistics into connections. Positive framing, no negativity about the festival experience.

## Copy

**Headline (large):**

> Your next dance partner might also need a ride.

**Subheadline:**

> WeDance connects festival dancers before you arrive. Carpools, roommates, group dinners, and more — all with people who dance like you do.

**CTA:**

> Scan to find your crew for [Festival Name]

**QR code** → landing page with UTM `?utm_source=poster&utm_campaign=[festival-slug]&utm_content=[location]`

**Footer:**

> Rides · Rooms · Dinners · Partners · Adventures

## Design Specs

| Element     | Value                                      |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Size        | A3 (297×420mm)                             |
| Orientation | Portrait                                   |
| Background  | Dark (black)                               |
| Font        | Poppins Bold (headline), Noto Sans (body)  |
| QR code     | Bottom-right, ~8cm                         |
| Colors      | White text, red (#F1023D) accents          |
| Placement   | Entrance, chill zone, hotel lobby          |
| Print qty   | 5-10 per festival                          |

## Accent Words (in red)

- "dance partner" in headline
- "also need a ride" in headline
- "your crew" in CTA
