# Policy: Decision-Making

**Date:** 2026-04-04
**Status:** Active

## Purpose
Alex and Kirill are equal partners (50/50). This policy defines how decisions are made at each level of the organization to avoid deadlocks, ensure speed, and maintain equivalence.

## Intended Outcome
Fast operational decisions by agents and role keepers. Strategic decisions by consent between the two founders. Clear escalation when disagreement arises.

## Policy Details

### Three decision levels:

**1. Operational (agent decides)**
Day-to-day work within the agent's role description and within the boundaries defined in Policy 001. No approval needed — just do the work.

Examples: drafting content, structuring data, updating backlogs, generating reports.

**2. Tactical (delegator decides)**
Decisions that affect a role's scope, priorities, or output quality. The agent's delegator (Alex or Kirill) decides.

Examples: approving a PR, approving outreach copy, reprioritizing the backlog, choosing which festival to target next.

**3. Strategic (partnership decides by consent)**
Decisions that affect the organization's direction, resources, or governance. Both founders must consent — meaning neither has a reasoned objection.

Examples: pivot/persevere on an experiment, adding a new domain, changing the business model, hiring, spending money, changing this policy.

### Consent process:
1. One partner proposes
2. The other reviews and raises any objections
3. If no reasoned objection → decision stands
4. If objection → discuss, amend, re-propose
5. If deadlock persists → time-box the discussion (1 week), then revisit with data

### Async by default:
Most decisions happen asynchronously. Strategic decisions that need discussion get a dedicated sync call. Weekly partnership check-in ensures nothing is stuck.

## Responsibilities

| Who | What |
|-----|------|
| Alex | Tactical decisions for Product Lead, Engineer, Operations Manager |
| Kirill | Tactical decisions for Designer, Partnership Manager, Marketing Lead |
| Partnership | Strategic decisions by consent |
| Agents | Operational decisions within their role, escalate the rest |

## Metrics and Monitoring

| Metric | How Monitored | Frequency |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Decision backlog | Unresolved strategic decisions older than 1 week | Weekly |
| Deadlocks | Partnership disagreements that stall progress | Per incident |
